Image files are gitignored. This catalog is the only file meant to be committed.

- **Instagram** `instagram.com/devx.network` is a different organization (flower logo, GDG/CCD content). Nothing from that account was saved.
- **TikTok** `@devx.network` is the right account (7 followers, 2 posts as of this pull). Both posts are the Nov 23 2025 promo: a text card and a 15s title-card video. No crowd footage.
- **YouTube** `@DEVxNetwork` has the long livestreams (Aug 2024 through 2026). Thumbnails are title cards. Short section downloads were blocked (HTTP 403 / web client returning images only), so no frames were pulled from the VODs. The edited recaps on Drive are the usable cut of that footage.
- **Notion.** "Photos & Video Capture" tasks on Weekend Function checklists are blank. No Google Photos or Dropbox album is linked. Video masters are the Drive folder above (`1rWQCKbUFC233YcAK29tNGfZgWst7ONUk`).
- **Personal Google Drive** and the other shared drives on this rclone remote are not DEVx and were not copied.
